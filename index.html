Bio

I am a PhD student at ISTA, advised by Krishnendu Chatterjee. I work on decision-making under uncertainty.

Two settings interest me. In the first, an agent has to act without seeing the full state of the system. In the second, the probabilities are not known exactly. You only know they lie in some set, and you have to plan against the worst case in that set. This is the problem also known as distributionally robust optimization.

I ask two things about these problems: can they be solved at all, and how fast. The answers turn into algorithms, and I write and benchmark them in C++ and Python, measuring how they scale on large randomly generated and structured instances and how they compare against existing solvers. More recently I have also worked on mechanism design for blockchain consensus.

Before ISTA I spent about ten years in competitive programming. Silver medal at the IOI, 33rd place at the ICPC World Finals, International Grandmaster on Codeforces with a peak rating of 2800. Alongside that I spent six years at Quera, Iran's largest technical education platform. I started by running contests and ended up managing a business line of about 40 people.
